Stock description STOCK

These are short summaries of the stock cars in the game. The power and times here are not the tuned figures. For tuned builds, see Tuned specs.

1988 Chevrolet Monte Carlo Super Sport STOCK

Stock summary. The tune on the specs page is 1,004 hp and 2.950 s to 60 mph.

In stock form this is Chevy’s late-1980s Super Sport sedan, named for Monte Carlo and raced as a NASCAR standard-bearer until the Lumina. 1988 was the last G-body year: a street and strip car, not the 1,004 hp tune.

2017 Acura NSX STOCK

Stock summary, about 600 hp. The tune is 853 hp and 2.450 s to 60 mph.

The stock 2017 NSX is a mid-engine hybrid supercar: a twin-turbo V6, three electric motors, all-wheel drive, about 600 hp, and 0–60 mph in a little over three seconds. That is not the 853 hp tune.

2011 BMW X5 M Forza Edition STOCK

Stock X5 M summary, about 547 hp and 4.7 s to 62 mph. The Forza Edition tune is 1,124 hp and 3.430 s to 60 mph.

The stock X5 M uses a twin-turbo 4.4-liter V8, about 547 hp, and reaches 62 mph in about 4.7 seconds, with 15.5-inch front brakes, the largest BMW had fitted at the time. The Forza Edition tune is a different, much faster build. Every page keeps the name 2011 BMW X5 M Forza Edition so the stock summary and the tune refer to the same car.
